NEON PROTOCOL — LEVEL 1
=======================
Open index.html directly in Chrome/Edge/Firefox.

Controls:
A/D or Arrow keys = move
W / Up / Space = jump
Mouse Left / J / K = shoot
E = recover transmission at the signal tower
Touch buttons are available on mobile.

Goal:
Clear every hostile in Level 1, reach the signal tower at the far right,
then press E to recover the transmission.

Level 1 includes:
- Designed platform map
- 12 enemies including ranged enemies and a guardian
- Pulse Pistol
- Neon SMG pickup
- Cash pickups
- Medical pickups
- Enemy projectiles
- No health regeneration
- Story intro and completion sequence
- No score system
